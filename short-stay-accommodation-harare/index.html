Short-stay accommodation in Harare

Need short-stay accommodation in Harare for a few nights? The Cosy Place welcomes stays from a couple of nights upward, with clear USD pricing for 2–10 night visits and a lower nightly rate from 11 nights.

Business travellers get fibre Wi-Fi, a quiet place to work from the lounge or dining table, and parking on site. Guests visiting family can cook their own meals and keep a private front door. Tourists and couples get a comfortable base near Westgate with backup power and borehole water.

Enquire with your dates on the homepage form to see an estimated total before you submit. We confirm availability quickly — typically within 2 hours. One-bedroom short stays are direct only; the whole cottage is also listed on Airbnb.
