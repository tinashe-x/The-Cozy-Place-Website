Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to direct booking enquiries and confirmed stays at The Cosy Place, Marlborough, Harare.

Enquiries vs confirmed bookings

Submitting the website form or messaging on WhatsApp is an enquiry, not a confirmed reservation. We reply with availability and the estimated total. A stay is confirmed only when we accept your dates and you accept the payment instructions we send.

Rates

Published rates are in USD per night: whole cottage $70 (2–10 nights) or $65 (11+ nights); one bedroom $50 or $45 for the same length bands. Taxes or platform fees may differ if you book via Airbnb (whole cottage only).

Cancellation

Direct bookings: free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. Later cancellations may forfeit the deposit as stated in your confirmation message. Airbnb bookings follow Airbnb’s cancellation policy.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 14:00. Check-out by 10:00 unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Property use

The cottage is private — you will not share it with another party. Maximum occupancy is 4 guests (whole cottage) or 2 guests (one bedroom). Please treat the home with care; unreasonable damage may be charged.
