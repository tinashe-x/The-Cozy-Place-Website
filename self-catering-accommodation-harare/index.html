Self-catering accommodation in Harare

Self-catering accommodation in Harare gives you space to cook, host family, and keep your own routine — without hotel restaurant mark-ups. The Cosy Place is built for that: a private cottage in Marlborough with a gas hob, oven, fridge, microwave, dining for six, and everyday kitchenware.

Utilities matter as much as the kitchen. Fibre Wi-Fi, solar backup for lighting, a solar geyser for hot water, and a private borehole help keep the stay comfortable when power or water is interrupted. Secure parking for two cars is on the property.

Compared with a hotel room, you get a lounge, covered porch and lawn to yourselves. Book both bedrooms or one bedroom; either option is exclusive use of the cottage. Rates are published in USD on our rates page.
