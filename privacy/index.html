Privacy Policy

This policy explains how The Cosy Place handles personal information you share when enquiring about a stay.

What we collect

When you use the booking form we receive your name, WhatsApp/contact number, optional email, stay type, dates, guest count, estimated total, and any message you send. WhatsApp chats are handled in WhatsApp’s own apps.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to availability requests, confirm bookings, and communicate about your stay. We do not sell your personal information.

Form processing

Website enquiries are delivered via Formspree. Their processing is subject to Formspree’s privacy terms. Analytics may be collected if Google Analytics is enabled on the site.

Retention

We keep enquiry and booking correspondence as long as needed to manage stays and resolve follow-ups, then delete or archive it when no longer required.
