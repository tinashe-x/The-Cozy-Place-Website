Two-bedroom cottage in Harare

The Cosy Place is a two-bedroom cottage in Marlborough, Harare, with two bathrooms, a lounge, dining area, kitchen, covered porch and lawn. The whole cottage sleeps up to four guests and is ideal for families, friends travelling together, or anyone who wants more space than a hotel suite.

Each bedroom has a double bed with white linen. Bathrooms have glass walk-in showers. Outside, you have private outdoor space and parking for two cars. Inside, DSTV and uncapped Wi-Fi are included.

If you only need one bedroom, you can book that option at a lower nightly rate while still having the kitchen and living areas to yourselves — the cottage is never split between two unrelated parties. See the floor plan schematic and USD rates.
