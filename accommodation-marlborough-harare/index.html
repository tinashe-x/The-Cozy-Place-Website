Accommodation in Marlborough, Harare

Looking for accommodation in Marlborough, Harare? The Cosy Place is a private self-catering cottage with its own entrance, porch and lawn — close to Westgate Shopping Centre and an easy drive to Avondale, Strathaven and Avonlea.

Guests choose Marlborough for quieter residential streets with shopping and services nearby. At the cottage you get a fully equipped kitchen, uncapped fibre Wi-Fi, DSTV, washer and dryer, solar inverter backup for lights, a solar geyser, and borehole water so taps keep running when municipal supply is unreliable.

Stay in the whole two-bedroom cottage (up to 4 guests) or book one bedroom at a lower rate (up to 2 guests). The home is never shared with another booking party.
