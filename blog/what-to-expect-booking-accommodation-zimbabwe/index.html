What to expect when booking accommodation in Zimbabwe

Many Harare stays quote rates in USD. At The Cosy Place, nightly prices are published on the site so you can estimate cost before you enquire. Submitting the form is a request for availability, not an instant charge.

We typically reply within 2 hours with availability and confirmation of the total. Payment is arranged after we confirm dates — usually bank transfer or another agreed method. Direct bookings allow free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in.

Ask about power and water when you book anywhere in Harare. This cottage includes a solar inverter for lights, a solar geyser, and a private borehole, plus uncapped Wi-Fi.

WhatsApp is widely used for coordination. Our manager number is South Africa-based (+27) and works well over WhatsApp internationally. Start on the booking form or message us with your dates.
