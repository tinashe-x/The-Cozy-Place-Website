A place of your own when you come home to Harare

You are visiting family and want your own front door. The Cosy Place is a private cottage in Marlborough, Harare.

The Cosy Place at a glance

  • Location: Marlborough, Harare — near Westgate, Avondale, Strathaven, and Avonlea
  • Whole cottage: 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, up to 4 guests — $70/night (2–10 nights) or $65 (11+ nights)
  • One bedroom: 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, up to 2 guests, still private — $50/night (2–10 nights) or $45 (11+ nights)
  • Included: kitchen, uncapped Wi-Fi, DSTV, solar inverter, solar geyser, borehole water, washer and dryer, parking for 2 cars
  • Book: direct on this site, or the whole cottage on Airbnb. From overseas, use the form or WhatsApp.

Can I book from overseas?

Yes. Use the form or WhatsApp. Rates are in USD.

What happens when the power or water drops?

Solar inverter for the lights, solar geyser for hot water, private borehole for the taps. Wi-Fi, DSTV, washer and dryer, and parking for two cars are included.

How do I get to family nearby?

The cottage is in Marlborough, near Westgate Shopping Centre. Avondale, Strathaven, and Avonlea are nearby. Parking for two cars is on the property.

Can I stay over the holidays?

Yes. The same USD rates apply. Send dates early.

Is this a shared guest house?

No. It is a private cottage. The whole cottage sleeps four. One bedroom sleeps two. Either way the cottage is not shared with another party.
