Accommodation in Marlborough, Harare

The Cosy Place is private self-catering accommodation in Marlborough, Harare, near Westgate Shopping Centre. It is a two-bedroom cottage for up to four guests, with its own entrance, porch, and lawn. Avondale, Strathaven, Westgate, and Avonlea are nearby.

The Cosy Place at a glance

  • Location: Marlborough, Harare — near Westgate, Avondale, Strathaven, and Avonlea
  • Whole cottage: 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, up to 4 guests — $59/night (2–10 nights) or $55 (11+ nights)
  • One bedroom: 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, up to 2 guests, still private — $39/night (2–10 nights) or $35 (11+ nights)
  • Included: kitchen, uncapped Wi-Fi, DSTV, solar inverter, solar geyser, borehole water, washer and dryer, parking for 2 cars
  • Book: direct on this site, or the whole cottage on Airbnb

Which part of Harare is the cottage in?

Marlborough. Avondale, Strathaven, Westgate, and Avonlea are nearby.

Is this self-catering accommodation?

Yes. You have the kitchen, with a gas hob, oven, fridge, and microwave, plus the lounge, dining room, both bedrooms, and both bathrooms.

How many guests can stay?

Up to four in the whole cottage, or up to two on the one-bedroom rate. Either way the cottage stays private — it is not shared with another party.

Can I book just one bedroom?

Yes. One bedroom and one bathroom is a lower direct rate for 1–2 guests. You still have the kitchen, lounge, porch, and lawn to yourselves. Book that option on our website; the Airbnb listing is set up for the whole cottage.

What happens when the power or water drops?

A solar inverter covers the lights, a solar geyser heats the water, and a private borehole keeps the taps running. Uncapped Wi-Fi, DSTV, and a washer and dryer are included, with parking for two cars.

Is this a shared guest house?

No. It is a private cottage with its own entrance, porch, and lawn. You do not share the rooms with other guests.
