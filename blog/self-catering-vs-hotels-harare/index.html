Self-catering vs hotels in Harare

Hotels suit short city trips when you want daily housekeeping and on-site restaurants. Self-catering suits guests who want a private front door, a kitchen, and room to host family without living in a lobby.

At The Cosy Place in Marlborough you get a lounge, dining table for six, full kitchen, porch and lawn. You cook when you want, store groceries, and keep the cottage to yourselves — whole two-bedroom or one-bedroom option.

Utilities are part of the comparison in Harare. The cottage includes fibre Wi-Fi, solar inverter backup for lights, a solar geyser, and borehole water. Secure parking for two cars is on site.
