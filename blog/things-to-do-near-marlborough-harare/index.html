Things to do near Marlborough, Harare

Marlborough is a practical base for errands and outings. Westgate Shopping Centre is nearby for groceries, pharmacies and everyday needs. Avondale, Strathaven and Avonlea are a short drive for cafés, services and visiting friends or family.

After a day out, The Cosy Place gives you a private porch and lawn, a kitchen to cook in, and a quiet lounge. Solar backup, borehole water and fibre Wi-Fi keep the cottage comfortable.
