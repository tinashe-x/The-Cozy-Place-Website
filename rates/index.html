Rates for self-catering accommodation in Harare

Direct booking rates at The Cosy Place are published in USD so you can plan before you enquire. Choose the whole cottage (both bedrooms and bathrooms, up to 4 guests) or the one-bedroom option (up to 2 guests). Either way the cottage is private — you do not share with another party.

Stay 2–10 nights 11+ nights
Whole cottage (2 bedrooms) $70 / night $65 / night
One bedroom $50 / night $45 / night

The booking form on the homepage shows an estimated total from these rates once you pick dates. We confirm availability and the final amount when we reply — usually within 2 hours.

Airbnb lists the whole cottage only. If you need the one-bedroom rate, book direct. Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in on direct bookings. Payment is arranged after availability is confirmed (USD bank transfer or another agreed method).
